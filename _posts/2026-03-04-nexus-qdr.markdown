The NEXUS Quick Data Releases are now available. They provide NIRCam and NIRSpec products from the NEXUS Deep observations and will be updated as additional epochs are completed.

The QDR data can be accessed at [this URL](https://ariel.astro.illinois.edu/nexus/qdr/). A description of the data products and their caveats is provided in the evolving [NEXUS Quick Release Notes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04586).

Please consult the Quick Release Notes for the latest release status before using the data.
