## EDR

The **Early Data Release (EDR)** covers the central 100 arcmin2 from the first partial NEXUS-Wide epoch. It includes:

- NIRCam imaging mosaics, uncertainty maps, and weight maps in F090W, F115W, F150W, F200W, F356W, and F444W.
- A NIRCam photometric source catalog.
- NIRCam WFSS one-dimensional spectra for bright sources (F356W or F444W < 21 mag) in F322W2 and F444W, together with quality-assurance products and visual-inspection flags.

[Access the EDR data](https://ariel.astro.illinois.edu/nexus/edr/) / [Read the EDR paper](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024arXiv241106372Z%2F/abstract)

## QDR

The **Quick Data Releases (QDR)** provide rapidly processed products from the ongoing NEXUS-Deep observations. They include:

- Epoch-by-epoch NIRCam F200W and F444W imaging mosaics and corresponding uncertainty maps.
- NIRSpec MSA/PRISM one-dimensional spectra for available Deep epochs.
- Quick-look and quality-assurance plots for visual inspection of the NIRSpec spectra.

QDR products are updated as additional Deep epochs are completed. Consult the Quick Release Notes for the latest release status and data caveats.

[Access the QDR data](https://ariel.astro.illinois.edu/nexus/qdr/) / [Read the Quick Release Notes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04586)
